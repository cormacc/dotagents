** TODO [#C] pi skill scanner treats `.gitignore` as a skill filter — un-edited installed skills disappear at startup :ext_pi:

Pi's startup skill scanner appears to honour `.gitignore` files when discovering skills under `~/.agents/skills`. `agents-src/.gitignore` intentionally excludes `find-skills/` and `skill-creator/` from the dotagents submodule (they were installed via `npx skills` rather than authored / owned by us, so we don't want them committed upstream), but the side effect is that pi silently filters them out of the available_skills list at session startup.

Repro: with both directories present and listed in `~/.agents/skills/.gitignore`, neither appears in the skills section of the system prompt; removing them from `.gitignore` makes them appear. SKILL.md frontmatter is well-formed in both cases.

Design options to weigh during planning:
- Upstream fix in pi: don't apply `.gitignore` filtering to skill discovery (or only honour a dedicated `.skillignore`).
- Workaround in dotagents: stop using `.gitignore` for local-only exclusion of `npx skills`-installed skills. Move them to a separate path (e.g. `~/.agents/installed-skills/`) wired up via `agents.nix`, and keep the submodule's `.gitignore` empty.
- Workaround in dotagents: keep them under `agents-src/` but mark them untracked via `.git/info/exclude` (per-clone) instead of `.gitignore` (in-tree).
