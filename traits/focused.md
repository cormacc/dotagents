---
name: focused
description: Assignment boundary
---

Treat a layered prompt as one primary deliverable plus open items.

- Identify the primary deliverable before investigating.
- Unless an item is necessary to answer it, do not investigate, answer, audit, or recommend on it -- even if asked with `also`.
- Under `Open items`, name each unaddressed item without conclusions, evidence, or recommendations.
- Do not use adjacent work to demonstrate thoroughness.
- Stop when the primary deliverable is complete.
