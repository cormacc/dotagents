* Intent

Evaluate how reusable Herdr/subagent choreography should be packaged without weakening the existing persona, result-envelope, and pane-ownership contracts.

* Summary

*Level:* exploration · *Tests:* none · *Docs:* inline

** Scope

*** In scope

- Whether multi-agent and pane/job workflows belong in skills or a declarative registry.
- Global versus project-local placement and the treatment of persistent agents and build/watch jobs.

*** Out of scope

- Implementing a workflow runner, schema, or new workflow definitions.
- Changing the Herdr or herdr-subagents contracts.

** Decisions

- Skills first; no workflow-definition format yet :: reusable, judgment-heavy choreography should be an individual skill which loads and composes =herdr= and =herdr-subagents=. Project-specific repeated choreography may be a project-local skill; ordinary development commands remain documented project commands.
  Rejected:
  - A dedicated workflow registry now: it would be unvalidated prose until it has a runner with typed parameters, dependency/wait semantics, result handling, ownership-scoped cleanup, and failure recovery.
  - Extending =.agents/subagents/=: that namespace is already exclusively the atomically shadowed persona and roster-override surface.
- Keep long-running jobs outside the subagent protocol :: build/CI/dev watchers run in direct, session-owned Herdr panes. They do not have the single-assignment result-envelope lifecycle required of a delegated subagent.
- Treat residents as exceptional session resources :: the current contract permits validated reuse only after completion and offers no generic re-task lifecycle. Do not make persistence a declarative primitive until that lifecycle is implemented.
- Reserve a separate workflow namespace only if repetition proves a declarative graph :: a future =herdr-workflows= capability may own a separate =.agents/workflows/= resolver and runner; it must not be folded into the subagent roster.

** Shipped

- Documented the evaluation conclusion: skill-based composition is the current implementation path; a declarative workflow system is deferred pending a validated execution contract.

* Implementation
