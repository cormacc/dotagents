* Intent

Act on the decisions taken at the gate of [[plan:2026-07-31-subagents-review-advisor-strategy-defaul.org]]: remove the mandatory pre-publish advisor consult and the =requires:= mechanism that expressed it, keep the advisor as an opt-in escalation at middle, retire =skilled-worker=, and re-document the advisor strategy against measured evidence rather than intuition.

* Summary

*Level:* MVP · *Tests:* existing suite green (410 tests / 2239 assertions) · *Docs:* SKILL + persona bodies

** Scope

*** In scope

- Remove =requires: advisor= from =worker.md= and roll back the =requires:= / =:required= mechanism introduced by commit =7b67e13= (=core.clj=, =cli.clj=, =contract.md=, =scripts/README.md=, =SKILL.md=, =core_test.clj=, =cli_test.clj=).
- Rewrite =worker.md='s advisor guidance: the pre-publish consult stops being mandatory and becomes an opt-in escalation for genuine difficulty.
- Retire =skilled-worker=: delete the definition and update its three =SKILL.md= references plus the roster-coupled assertion in =cli_test.clj=.
- Rewrite =SKILL.md= § Advisor strategy, and add tier guidance recording that light is the efficient default and feather a false economy for implementation work.
- Document the advisor-tier override convention, including that it is defeated when an executor bypasses the CLI.

*** Out of scope

- Changing =advisor.md='s =model: middle=; the gate decided the tier stays as it is.
- A structured parent-side advisor-tier flag; the gate ruled the existing convention adequate and a flag merely ergonomic.
- Mechanical enforcement of consults. The evaluation concluded the mandate could not be enforced, which is why it is being removed rather than hardened.
- The dispatch defect tracked separately as task =d4bd209f=.

** Decisions

- Roll back rather than repurpose =requires:= :: the mechanism exists solely to express the mandatory advisor consult, and =worker= is its only declarant. With the mandate gone it has no user, and keeping an unused capability-mandate concept would cost prompt tokens and reader attention for nothing. Rejected: retaining it undeclared for future personas — speculative, and cheap to reintroduce from =7b67e13= if ever wanted.
- Retire =skilled-worker= rather than keep a hard no-advisor guarantee :: an optional capability that goes unused is not a meaningful difference from one that is absent, and the roster is easier to reason about with a single executor persona parameterised by =--model=. Rejected: keeping it so =advisor= is absent from =spawns:= entirely.
- Keep the advisor at middle :: the one equal-executor comparison favoured middle (35/35 at $5.3666) over heavy (34/35 at $7.1269); =--model heavy= stays available per spawn for extremis, which was never actually exercised in the evaluation.

** Gotchas

- A retired frontmatter key must be *inert*, not an error. Personas live in a three-tier resolution chain including unmanaged =~/.agents/subagents/=, so a hand-written definition may still declare =requires:=; =ignores-retired-requires-frontmatter-key= pins that it is ignored without perturbing spawn resolution.
- =cli_test.clj= couples to the live packaged roster, so adding or retiring a persona breaks assertions there. Retiring one is not a pure deletion: update the weight table and assert the absence, or the coupling silently rots.

** Risks

- Removing the mandate may slow weak executors: the feather trio showed duration falling 3047.8s → 854.7s as advisor involvement rose. Accepted — n=1 on the noisiest metric, feather is deprecated for this work, and the opt-in escalation path remains for a genuinely stuck worker.

* Implementation

** Landed

*Mechanism rollback (ad7c6ecf).* Reversed commit =7b67e13='s mechanism only. =core.clj=: =resolve-required= and its comment block removed. =cli.clj=: =delegation-guidance= restored to single arity, =prompt-text= no longer threads =required=, =spawns-policy= reverted to returning the resolved policy directly, and =:required= dropped from the =--print-prompt= preview, the ledger entry, and both =prompt-text= call sites. =contract.md= and =scripts/README.md= paragraphs removed; =SKILL.md='s "Granted capability and mandated capability are separate" paragraph removed.

Test coverage was updated rather than deleted. =core_test.clj='s =resolve-required-contract= became =ignores-retired-requires-frontmatter-key=, which pins the more useful property now: a stale =requires:= key in a hand-written or third-party persona must be *inert* rather than an error, and must not perturb the resolved spawn policy. The pinned =delegation-guidance= assertions gained a case proving an =advisor= in the allow-list is covered by the ordinary gap-only clause, plus an =ArityException= assertion so the two-arity mandate variant cannot quietly return. =cli_test.clj='s =worker-mandate-drops-with-the-effective-allow-list= became =worker-spawn-policy-narrows-to-a-leaf=, retaining both narrowing paths (=--spawns none= and depth-forced) while asserting the ledger carries no =:required= key.

*Persona changes (c66a8c1d, 3e8af8f0).* =worker.md= lost =requires: advisor=, and its step 7 "Consult before publishing" is gone — the workflow is now seven steps ending at *Report*. Its § Advisor consultation opens by stating there is no routine pre-publish review and why, names the two genuine triggers (debugging dead end after repeated failures; materially ambiguous high-stakes decision), keeps the focused-context budget and the soft cap of three, and directs a stuck worker to publish =BLOCKED= rather than keep spending. The =description:= no longer advertises advisor consultation as a defining feature. =skilled-worker.md= was removed with =git rm=; its three =SKILL.md= references and the roster-coupled weight table in =cli_test.clj= were updated, the latter gaining a positive assertion that the file is absent.

*Documentation (93149919).* § Advisor strategy became § Executor tier and advisor strategy: one executor persona parameterised by =--model=, an opt-in middle advisor for a stuck worker with =--model heavy= for a high-stakes call, the light-default and feather-false-economy guidance with measured figures, and the override convention documented together with its failure mode — a worker that hand-rolls a consult via raw =herdr agent start= inherits the default model, spends outside the ledger, and orphans the pane, so ledger consult counts are a floor rather than the truth. The superseded 2026-07-29 record is marked as such instead of carrying its stale rename note.
