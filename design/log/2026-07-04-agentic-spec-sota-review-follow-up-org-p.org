* Intent
Selectively adopt the two highest-value, philosophy-compatible ideas from the mid-2026 spec-driven SOTA review — spec↔acceptance/test linkage and an AGENTS.md-as-constitution planning check — plus smaller tooling/convention enhancements, while keeping the nudge-not-gate, contract-first, git-native core of org-plan/org-tasks intact.

* Summary
*Level:* production · *Tests:* thorough (for `ot` changes; doc-only tasks none) · *Docs:* inline (the skills are the docs)

Follow-up work programme from the SOTA review preserved at [[file:2026-07-04-agentic-spec-sota-review-follow-up-org-p/research-spec-driven-sota.md][research-spec-driven-sota.md]] (same-stem support folder). The review found the local model ahead of SOTA on git-nativeness, tool-agnosticism, planning craft, and the don't-duplicate-the-contract philosophy, and behind on spec→test coupling, drift enforcement, constitution/steering, and tooling-verified discovery. This record plans the selective borrowings, tiered HIGH → MEDIUM → LOW, and records what we explicitly decline to adopt.

- Making `ot` a workflow engine (phases, gates, model calls).

** Decisions
- Selective borrowing, tiered HIGH → MEDIUM → LOW per the review's prioritisation :: constitution check is cheap/doc-only; spec↔test linkage is the biggest-value gap-closer; tooling (spec list, stale advisory) follows; GWT and link-syntax broadening are deferred polish.
- The 2026-06-04 OpenSpec evaluation's conclusions still hold ([[file:2026-06-04-evaluate-openspec-vs-org-plan-skill-for.org][record]]): no merge engine, couple to existing contracts, warnings-only enforcement. SOTA has added polish and enforcement rhetoric since, not reasons to reverse.
- NOT adopting a generative `specs/` corpus + delta-merge engine (Spec Kit/OpenSpec core) :: rebuilding a parallel spec corpus that must be regenerated/merged manufactures a new drift surface that the "couple to existing contracts" philosophy specifically avoids; in this repo the skills *are* the specs.
- NOT adopting hard CI merge gates on spec drift :: contradicts the deliberate nudge-not-gate stance and the solo/small-team context; the advisory `ot doctor` path is the ceiling. Reassess only for a high-assurance contract layer.
- NOT adopting the spec-first "code serves spec" inversion (Spec Kit's central claim) :: for a repo whose deliverable is the contract (skills), the inversion is redundant — contract and code are one artifact.
- NOT adopting IDE-locked steering (Kiro) :: violates tool-agnosticism. The constitution role goes to the already-implicit AGENTS.md + skills instead of a new file.

** Risks
- Advisory fatigue: each new `ot doctor` warning class risks being ignored like lint noise; accepted — keep findings few, precise, and closure-scoped.
- "Declared-but-stale" heuristics (git-scope diff of code vs spec) may false-positive on incidental co-location; accepted for an advisory, tune on evidence.
- Constitution check could decay into a checkbox ritual; mitigated by wording it as a named drafting practice with concrete questions, not a template section.
