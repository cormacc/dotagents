* Intent
Replace repository-dependent trait scenarios with synthetic subjects and record marginal effects honestly. Separate a fragment's write-boundary behaviour from restrictions imposed by the probe harness.

* Summary
*Level:* prototype · *Tests:* thorough · *Docs:* inline

Focused and grounded synthetic scaffold pairs are recorded: focused produced the required treated/control response difference; grounded did not discriminate. The existing native Codex runner uses a read-only sandbox and direct prompts; it is not an exact packaged-persona Pi run and does not establish the deployed Pi instruction baseline. A no-write outcome from that sandbox cannot by itself demonstrate the read-only fragment's effect.

** Scope
*** In scope
- Synthetic focused and grounded scenarios, pre-registered conditions and one treated/control pair per scenario.
- Directory-form fragments with byte-identical prompt contents and user-layer gate records.
- A disposable write-enabled read-only probe design and explicit baseline verification.
*** Out of scope
- Weakening a recorded condition, claiming general equivalence from one pair, or treating a pass as an admission prerequisite.
- Rewriting or deleting a fragment based only on the current limited harness.
- Letting a write-boundary probe mutate the real checkout or external state.

** Acceptance
*** Core functionality
- [X] Focused and grounded have synthetic gates and honestly stated paired results.
- [X] Prompt moves preserve the fragment bytes and resolution behaviour.
- [ ] Read-only has a positive mutation control on a disposable target, followed by a paired write-enabled probe.
*** Edge cases
- [ ] Exact persona, model/provider and baseline instruction loading are stated for each observation.
- [X] Non-discriminating results remain explicit; no condition is relaxed after the run.
*** Anti-criteria
- [ ] Must not: infer a trait effect from sandbox-enforced refusal.
- [ ] Must not: use this repository as the reviewed subject or expose a recorded answer key as task context.

** Decisions
- Use the existing read-only runner for recommendation/review scaffold observations only :: focused and grounded have observable response boundaries. Rejected:
  - Treat the same runner as a read-only mutation test: both arms have an independent prohibition on writes.
  - Claim exact Pi deployment coverage from native Codex: instruction loading and persona composition differ.
- Keep the whole task open until read-only and the baseline boundary are measured :: the current partial observations may guide follow-up but cannot satisfy the original task's full acceptance criteria.
