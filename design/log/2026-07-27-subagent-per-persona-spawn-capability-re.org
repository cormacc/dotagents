* Intent

Replace the hard-coded planner-only delegation exception with declared per-persona spawn capability: a `spawns:` frontmatter allow-list resolved at spawn time (flag > frontmatter > default deny), injected into the child, and enforced mechanically by the CLI. One nesting level absolutely — anything spawned below the root is a leaf — and below-root spawns stay blocking and one-at-a-time.

* Summary

*Level:* MVP · *Tests:* thorough · *Docs:* SKILL/contract/README

** Scope
*** In scope
- `spawns:` persona frontmatter parsing, validation, and precedence resolution (`--spawns` flag > frontmatter > default deny).
- Mechanical capability and depth-bound enforcement at `run`/`start` via injected `HERDR_SUBAGENT_SPAWNS`.
- Generalised `delegation-guidance` prompt composition and nested pane labels for any spawning persona.
- Ledger `:spawns` / `:spawns-source` fields and `--print-prompt` reporting.
- Roster grants for `planner` and `worker`; SKILL.md leaf-rule rewrite; contract.md spawn-gating section; CLI usage text.
*** Out of scope
- Below-root parallel fan-out, waves-of-two below root, or any cardinality relaxation (root privilege unchanged).
- A `review-lead` / synthesising-reviewer persona (parked follow-up).
- Mechanical enforcement of below-root one-at-a-time cardinality (stays prompt/doctrine strength).
- Envelope format, retro gating semantics, resident lifecycle, Herdr changes, or the vendored upstream Herdr skill.
- New live smoke legs.

** Decisions
- Resolve-then-inject, mirroring retro gating :: the parent resolves policy once at spawn (flag > frontmatter > default deny), records it, and injects it; the child CLI enforces from its own env at `run`/`start`, before preflight, ledger allocation, and pane mutation.
  Rejected:
  - Prompt-only enforcement (current planner strength): spawn failure modes are billable and pane-creating; a mechanical check is one env read.
  - Child self-resolution from the roster at invocation: cannot carry a per-spawn flag override, and mid-flight roster edits would change a live child's capability.
- Default deny :: capability is declared per persona in the roster, visible and auditable.
  Rejected:
  - Default-allow: billable/pane-creating failure modes, and grandchild findings reach the root through two layers of testimony (SKILL.md § Trusting a result).
- Depth bound via forced-empty injection :: any below-root spawn injects `HERDR_SUBAGENT_SPAWNS=""` and leaf guidance; no depth counter.
  Rejected:
  - A depth-counter env var: more state for the same one-level result.
  - Label-slash inference (a child reading its own pane label): labels are operator-mutable display metadata, manual starts under-report nesting, and the label never carries information the injected env lacks. `nested-prefix`'s anchored-regex throw remains an undesigned backstop only.
- Single value-bearing `--spawns` with literal `none` :: one flag covers grant and deny overrides.
  Rejected:
  - A `--no-spawn` boolean: couples into the shared `boolean-flags`/`help-request?` set for no expressiveness gain.
  - Frontmatter-only (no flag): the motivating cases are one-off grants (a worker needing a scout this time) that should not require roster edits.
- Fail fast on unresolvable persona names in both flag and frontmatter :: mirrors the `retro:` invalid-value precedent; a typo silently degrading to leaf surfaces as a confusing refusal deep in a delegation.
- Roster grants: `planner` and `worker`; `reviewer` stays a leaf :: worker factual gaps are the strongest motivating case; a reviewer needing a repro scout is a one-off `--spawns scout` until it recurs.
  Rejected:
  - `spawns: reviewer` for a synthesising lead reviewer: requires below-root parallel fan-out (a separate constitution change), adds a lossy trust hop on exactly the findings the root must verify against source, strands grandchild panes owned by a dead lead, and a self-referential grant changes every ordinary reviewer's composed guidance. Parked as follow-up.
